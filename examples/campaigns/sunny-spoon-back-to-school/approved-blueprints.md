# Approved Post Blueprints

## BP-001 — One Less School-Night Decision

Awareness and engagement through a relatable parent moment. Use a question hook, very light promotion, a conversation CTA, and a realistic after-school family visual.

## BP-002 — What’s in the Box?

Consideration through clear contents, serving size, price, and availability. Use “Four people. One box.” with a product-photo layout and a view-the-offer CTA.

## BP-003 — Dinner Is Ready When You Need a Plan

Warm conversion through a full-day family moment. Use a welcoming offer presentation, moderate promotion, and the direct order CTA without false urgency.

All three blueprints were approved after BP-003 was revised to feel warmer and less sales-focused.

# Visual Guide

Use sunshine yellow, dark green, and cream. Official HEX values are not yet confirmed, so do not invent them.

Prefer realistic family meals, believable food close-ups, warm evening light, natural expressions, uncluttered layouts, and restrained brand-color accents.

Avoid luxury dining, over-staged scenes, exaggerated reactions, artificial-looking food, generated packaging text, and generated logos. Apply an approved logo asset separately when supplied.

Provide useful alt text and keep overlay areas readable with adequate contrast.

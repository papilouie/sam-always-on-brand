# Voice Guide

Sunny Spoon sounds warm, neighborly, practical, and lightly playful. Use everyday language and short to medium sentences. Write as “we.” Keep promotion friendly and specific.

## Boundaries

- Playful, but not childish.
- Warm, but not overly sentimental.
- Practical, but not dry.

## Approved

“Busy day? Dinner can still be the easy part.” This begins with a familiar moment and keeps the promise modest.

“Four people, one box, and one less weekday decision.” This is clear and lightly playful.

## Avoid

“Good food, fewer dinner negotiations.” This makes the family dynamic too much of the joke.

Avoid aggressive capitalization, false urgency, parenting guilt, and unsupported superlatives.

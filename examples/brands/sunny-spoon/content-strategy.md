# Content Strategy

## Easier family moments

Show how Sunny Spoon reduces everyday dinner friction without suggesting that convenience makes someone a better parent.

Suggested topics include school-night routines, simple meal decisions, shared family time, and practical ordering information.

## What is in the box

Answer concrete buying questions using confirmed product details. Avoid unverified savings comparisons.

## Local community

Use friendly questions and familiar Austin family moments without claiming community partnerships that are not documented.

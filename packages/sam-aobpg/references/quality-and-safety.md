# Quality, claims, accessibility, and batch review

## Evidence lanes

Before blueprints and before delivery, classify material as:

- **Confirmed:** explicitly supplied or present in approved source material.
- **Interpretation:** reasonable reading that must not become a hard fact.
- **Missing:** useful information not yet available.
- **Requires evidence:** a claim that should not appear without support.

Offer a safe alternative when evidence is unavailable. Never fabricate firsthand use for affiliate content.

## Brand and campaign review

Check voice, audience, goal, core message, offer, CTA, prohibited phrases, visual rules, platform treatment, and required disclosures. Report meaningful issues only. Use plain `Ready`, `Revise`, or `Blocked` language rather than invented precision.

## Batch variation

Compare openings, angles, benefits, structures, evidence, CTAs, visuals, and hashtags. Required product names and disclosures may repeat. If concepts are strategically alike, revise them before approval or delivery.

## Accessibility

For visual deliverables, provide functional alt text describing meaningful content rather than decorative marketing language. Do not rely on color alone. Suggest readable overlays and contrast. For video, include captions or on-screen equivalents for essential spoken information.

## Sensitive or regulated topics

Flag medical, legal, financial, political, safety-critical, or regulated claims for appropriate verification and review. Do not present SAM's guidance as legal approval. Preserve the user's authorized scope and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal data.

## Affiliate and sponsorship content

Identify the relationship, whether actual use is confirmed, supported benefits and limitations, required disclosure wording, and destination link. Place disclosures prominently enough to travel with the content.

## Final delivery check

Confirm:

- Brand Pack and Campaign Brief are the approved versions.
- Required blueprints were approved.
- Deliverable count matches the production plan and is no more than twelve.
- Every claim is confirmed or clearly qualified.
- Platform adaptations are meaningful.
- Visual outputs include accessibility content.
- No unresolved blocking placeholder remains unless explicitly accepted.
- The final menu offers clear next actions.

# Platform and format playbooks

Use current platform constraints when tools can verify them. When exact limits are not verified, avoid claiming precise maximums and write conservatively.

## Facebook

Use conversational context, natural paragraphs, community relevance, one clear CTA, and few or no hashtags. Story-led copy may be longer when the idea warrants it.

## Instagram

Lead with a strong first line, use scannable short paragraphs, visual-first language, brand-approved emoji behavior, selective hashtags, and a CTA compatible with confirmed link placement.

## LinkedIn

Use professional but human language, a clear insight or lesson, credible context, restrained promotion, and an appropriate discussion or business CTA. Avoid hollow thought-leadership clichés.

## TikTok and short video

Open immediately with a spoken or visual hook. Use short, conversational lines, scene progression, on-screen text, caption, spoken CTA, duration estimate, and disclosure placement.

## YouTube

Provide title options, opening hook, structured script, description, CTA, thumbnail concept, chapters when useful, and search-oriented wording only when it remains natural.

## X

Focus on one clear idea with concise setup. Use a thread only when the content needs it. Avoid unnecessary hashtags.

## Threads

Favor conversational, personal, or community language and a natural response invitation. Avoid overly polished advertising copy.

## Pinterest

Use a search-friendly title, clear benefit, descriptive copy, strong visual concept, and destination-focused CTA.

## Newsletter and article

Provide subject or headline options, preview text when relevant, a purposeful opening, clear sections, evidence, visual-placement suggestions, and one primary CTA.

## Image post

Specify visual objective, subject, setting, composition, lighting, mood, brand colors, graphic style, negative space, text overlay, logo placement, negative instructions, aspect-ratio suggestion, and alt text. Apply official logos after generation rather than asking an image model to recreate them.

## Carousel

Define cover, slide progression, final CTA, caption, and accessibility summary. Do not split one repetitive caption across multiple slides.
